Questions

How do I start a new note?

In the library, press + and choose Add Note. It opens in the room around you, with a menu in front of you.

How do I add things to a note?

Use the menu in front of you. Its buttons add a title, a note card, a graph, a line or a photo.

How do I move something?

Drag the bar under it. Turn your wrist while dragging to tilt it, and press Straighten in its settings to level it again.

I can't find something I placed.

Tap it if you can see it, then press Bring to View in its settings to bring it in front of you.

How do I see a graph in 3D?

Fill in its bars and press Done. Drag the graph to turn it, and pinch with both hands to resize it. Tap it to change its values.

How do I bend a line?

Tap the line to open its settings, then drag the dot in its middle. Drag the handle at either end to put that end anywhere.

How do I use folders?

In the library, press + and choose Add Folder. Drag a note or a folder onto a folder to put it inside, or choose Move to in its menu. A folder's menu also changes its color and cover.

Where are my notes saved?

On your device, in the app's own storage. MocaNote doesn't sync between devices, and deleting the app deletes your notes.

Can I get back a note I deleted?

No. The app can't restore a deleted note, so check before you confirm.

Does MocaNote collect my data?

No. It has no account, no analytics and no servers. See the Privacy Policy.

What do I need to use MocaNote?

An Apple Vision Pro running visionOS 26 or later.

I found a bug. What should I send?

Email what happened, the steps that lead to it, and your visionOS version. Screenshots help a lot.
