MocaNote

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Support

MocaNote is an app for Apple Vision Pro, made and published by Justin Yu. This policy explains how it handles your information.

Summary

MocaNote does not collect, store on any server, or share your personal data. Everything you make stays on your device.

Your notes

Your notes, titles, graphs, lines, photos, folders and covers are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They aren't sent to me or to anyone else. There's no account to create.

Photos

When you add a picture, you choose it in Apple's system photo picker. MocaNote receives only the pictures you choose, and keeps its own copy on your device so that it can show them. It can't see the rest of your library.

Your position in the room

MocaNote uses your head position to place items in front of you and turn them to face you. This is used only while the app is running. It stays on your device and isn't saved or shared.

Analytics and advertising

MocaNote has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The app doesn't connect to any server, and I don't operate any.

Deleting your data

When you delete a note or a folder, it's moved aside on your device and permanently removed after 30 days. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

MocaNote collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date.
